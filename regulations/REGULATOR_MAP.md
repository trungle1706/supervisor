# Regulator map for AI systems in a Vietnamese bank (WP-W0)

> **Not legal advice. Bank counsel must confirm.** The map lists which instruments, from which regulator, bind or guide an AI system in a bank, what RegKB stores, and what is missing. It was made on 2026-10-28 from vbpl.vn (title and number search, sitemap search, full-text fetch) and from the stored texts. Absence from a keyword search is not proof that a document does not exist.

The stored rows were produced from `regulations/vn/*.yaml` and `regulations/intl/*.yaml` on 2026-10-28 (counts come from the data); the missing rows and the gap column are written by hand. Keep it in step when you add an instrument. Run `python -m supervisor.regkb coverage --levels` for control coverage per instrument. A test (`tests/regkb/test_regulator_map.py`) checks the stored rows against the files.

## How to read the gap column

Each stored Vietnamese row has a short list of articles that are not obligations yet. They were found by comparing the article headings of the stored text with the article numbers in the rows (`article_ref`). A listed article is a candidate, not a finding that it binds the bank. Clause-level gaps inside an article that already has a row are only listed where the research note (`docs/phase12/research/regulation-structure.md`, section 3) found them.
